# Hypit — directed generated-media video

Distilled from `hypit` (MIT). Own words throughout.

## Give the work useful structure
- Start from the brief, references, or supplied material — never from a blank
  assumption about the topic. Name the audience, the one idea, the duration,
  and the ratio before directing anything.
- Structure the video as scenes with a clear purpose each: what the viewer
  learns or feels in that scene, and what carries them to the next one.

## Direct the material
- Direct generated media the way a director directs a shoot: subject, action,
  setting, light, camera, and mood are all stated, not left to defaults.
- Author components (repeatable visual units) rather than one-off prompts
  where a look must recur. A component that appears twice is defined once and
  reused verbatim.
- Prepare for the work at hand: check what media, references, and credentials
  actually exist before promising a look that needs them.

## Understand and adapt
- Read the supplied material before composing. If a reference contradicts the
  brief, say so and ask which wins — do not silently pick one.
- Adapt the treatment to the material: a talking-head brief, a product demo,
  and a story need different pacing, not the same template.

## Compose and refine
- Compose scene by scene, then watch the assembly as a whole: pacing, visual
  continuity, and whether the one idea survives the full duration.
- Refine by recomposing the weak scene, not by rewording the whole video.
  A global change invalidates prior approval of the affected scenes.

## Standing responsibilities
- **Voice continuity:** one frozen voice profile for the whole video,
  repeated verbatim in every clip (see stickman-director.md — the same
  contract applies here).
- **No invented facts:** no unsupported statistics, quotations, or product
  claims in narration or on screen.
- **No visible text by default:** generated clips default to no visible
  words, letters, numbers, or interface copy; short overlays are a separate
  post-production decision.
- **Continuity is checked, not assumed:** match each clip ending to the next
  opening through action, prop, screen direction, or a planned cut.
