# Repurposing + advocacy + interviewer — adapt, amplify, start from the story

Distilled from `linkedin-skills` (MIT bundle: repurposer, employee-advocacy,
interviewer). Own words; reference files distilled, not copied.

## Repurposing (another platform → native LinkedIn)
- Take the source (a post, video, or article) and adapt it into a **native**
  LinkedIn post — not a link with a caption, not a pasted transcript.
- Keep the source's one idea; rebuild the structure for LinkedIn (hook,
  short lines, close). Credit the source where the idea is not the user's.
- If the source's claims cannot be verified, they do not survive the move —
  a repurposed invention is still an invention.
- Return as a copy-paste block. The owner posts it himself.

## Employee advocacy (a team, not a megaphone)
- An advocacy program gives a team **angles and drafts**, not scripts to
  paste identically. Each person posts in their own voice, from their own
  experience, on their own account — with their own approval.
- One idea, many voices: the program supplies the idea, the proof point, and
  the hook formula; each draft is still individual, still audited, still
  posted by its owner.
- Never post to anyone's account for them. Never schedule on their behalf
  without their explicit word for that post.

## Interviewer (start here if there is nothing to say)
- **Working out what the user actually has to say, or having nothing concrete
  for a draft to use, is the first workflow — not a failure mode.** Interview
  the user and keep the answers in a **story bank**, which every writing
  workflow reads.
- Ask one question at a time about real experience: a project, a failure, a
  decision, a number that changed, a lesson that cost something. Follow the
  answer, not a questionnaire.
- The voice profile needs posts the user already wrote; the story bank only
  needs a career. Start here if the user has never posted.
- A story bank entry is a fact with a source (the user's words, the date) —
  never an embellished version. Drafts may only use what the bank holds or
  what the user states in the session; anything else is `[N]`.
