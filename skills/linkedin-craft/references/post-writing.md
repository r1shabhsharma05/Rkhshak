# Post writing — hooks, structure, extraction (draft only)

Distilled from `linkedin-skills` (MIT bundle: post-writer, hook-extractor).
Own words; the bundle's 81+ reference files are distilled, not copied.

## The post-writer flow
1. **Take the material.** The user's real experience, from the story bank or
   stated in this conversation. If there is nothing concrete, stop and ask —
   a draft with no material is a draft full of inventions.
2. **Pick the structure before the words.** A post is a formula with the
   user's facts in it, not a pile of sentences. Pick one hook formula and one
   body structure; say which, in one line, before drafting.
3. **Draft.** Short lines, one idea per line, plain words. The hook earns the
   read; the body earns the comment; the close earns the follow.
4. **Audit.** Run the humanizer audit (`humanizer-audit.md`) before showing
   the draft as final. A draft that fails audit is rewritten, not shipped.
5. **Return as a copy-paste block.** The owner posts it himself. Never post.

## Hook formulas (structure, not text)
- **Rejection-count open:** a number of rejections/attempts, the turn, the
  lesson. Only with the user's real count — never borrow someone else's.
- **False-binary dissolve:** "Everyone says X or Y. The truth is Z." The Z
  must be something the user actually learned, not a platitude.
- **Anecdote-meets-evidence bridge:** one concrete story, then the number or
  pattern that generalises it. The number must be real or marked `[N]`.
- **Diverging-curves close:** two paths over time, the choice that separated
  them, the lesson. Works when the user actually lived both sides.
- **Controlled A/B anecdote:** the same situation handled two ways, the
  outcome that differed, the principle. Both halves must be real.
- **Founder formulas** (see `profile-and-planning.md`): reprice the category,
  content-to-pipeline, audience of one, the scarce-shots math, the
  unglamorous bet, the delegation line, the learning gate.

## Hook extraction (from a viral post)
- Extract the **formula**: the structural moves (open, turn, evidence,
  close) and why each earns attention. Quote at most a short fragment to show
  the move; never reproduce the post.
- **Never copy the post with a new name on it.** A draft built from an
  extracted formula uses the user's own facts. If the user has no matching
  experience, say so and ask for their real material.
- A hook formula describes logic, not topic. Two posts can share a formula
  and say opposite things — the formula is not the content.

## Rules
- One idea per post. A post with two ideas is two posts, one of them wasted.
- No invented traction: no user counts, revenue, offers, or rejections the
  user did not evidence. Placeholders (`[N]`) beat invented numbers.
- No viral-bait that the body cannot cash: the hook promises only what the
  body delivers.
