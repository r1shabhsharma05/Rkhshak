# Research Gate — deep research, reviewed by the whole team

Research happens before any plan and before any build. The point is not
volume — it is a verdict the whole team can stand behind.

## The research note (posted to the shared board)
Every note carries the same sections, in this order. A missing section
is a gap, and a gap is named, not hidden.

1. **Goal (one line).** What the owner asked for, in the owner's words.
2. **Scope.** What is in, what is out, what is explicitly undecided.
3. **Market.** Who else does this, what they do well, what they miss.
   Named competitors, not "many apps exist".
4. **Future.** Where this is heading — is the idea early, on time, or
   late? What changes in the next year that could help or kill it?
5. **Viability.** Will it work or not, and why. Evidence, not optimism.
6. **Risks.** The three biggest ways it fails, each with a likelihood
   and what would warn us early.
7. **Best-in-market bar.** What "best" concretely means here — the
   feature, quality, or result that beats the named competitors — and
   what it would take to reach it.
8. **Sources.** Every number, claim, and competitor note cites its
   source (link, doc, or observation). No source, no claim.
9. **Verdict.** GO / GO WITH GAPS (gaps named) / NO-GO (reason named).
   A NO-GO with a reason is a success — it stopped weak work early.

## The gate — every agent reviews, unanimous pass
Each role reviews the note against its own lens and posts one verdict:
PASS, or FAIL with the named gap.

| Role | Lens — the question it must answer |
|---|---|
| Coder | Buildable? Can this be built with the tools and time we have? What is the hardest technical part? |
| Editor | Shippable? Can this be shown, explained, or posted honestly? Is there anything to show? |
| Job Applier | Relevant? Does this change what roles or skills matter? (Silence is a pass — a role with nothing to say says PASS, not nothing.) |
| Agency Owner | Sellable? Would a customer pay for this? Who, and why them? |
| Verifier | Testable? Can each outcome be checked end-to-end? What cannot be tested, and why? |
| Planner | Plannable? Does the note contain enough to write steps with owners and done-criteria? |
| Master | Complete? Every section present, every claim sourced, verdict explicit? |

- **PASS needs unanimity.** One FAIL is a failed gate, not a minority
  opinion. The failing agent names the gap in one line.
- **Silence is not a pass.** Every role posts a verdict. A role that
  has nothing to add posts `PASS — no objection from <lens>`.
- **The Master records the verdicts** on the board with the round
  number. No verdict, no next step.

## The cap — 2 research rounds, then escalate
- **Round 1 fail:** the Researcher goes one round deeper, addressing
  exactly the named gaps. No new scope, no re-research of passed parts.
- **Round 2 fail:** the Master stops and escalates to the owner with
  the open questions, the two rounds of evidence, and the options
  (narrow the scope / accept the gap / kill the idea). The owner
  decides. The team never loops a third round on its own.
- **A NO-GO verdict** skips the gate: the Master takes it straight to
  the owner with the reason. The owner may still order the work — that
  is their call, recorded on the board.

## What "deep and hard" means, concretely
- More than one angle: the market angle, the user angle, the technical
  angle, the future angle. One angle is a guess.
- Competitors named and checked, not assumed. A competitor's claim is
  verified against its own page or product, never copied from a list.
- The future section names a change that could kill the idea. If no
  such change exists, say so — "no credible threat found" is a finding,
  not a gap.
- Numbers carry sources. A number without a source is removed at the
  gate, not footnoted later.
