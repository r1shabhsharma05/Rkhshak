# Plan and voice

## The control room
Everything else in this skill executes; this decides what gets executed. Run it once a week, on the same day.

## Input files (read them if they exist)
- **Voice profile** (`~/.claude/instagram/voice.md`): how the user talks on camera, what they never say, who they are talking to. If it does not exist, ask for three of the user's own Reels, transcribe or read them, infer the voice, and write the file. A script in the wrong voice is unusable — they have to say it out loud.
- **Swipe file** (`~/.claude/instagram/swipe.md`): the user's own evidence from the research workflow about which formulas are landing in their niche right now. It outranks anything in this skill.
- **Log** (`~/.claude/instagram/log.md`): which hook formula each post used, and when. It stops the plan repeating a theme from the last fortnight.

If none exist, ask for four things and write them down: what the user sells and to whom; the 3–4 themes they want to be known for; what actually happened this week (a client call, a number, a mistake, a thing they built, an argument); ten accounts worth being visible to.

## What to post
4–5 posts a week, at least 3 of them Reels. Reels are the only format that reliably reaches people who do not follow the account. Carousels go deep with the people who already do. Stories are daily and planned separately.

| Type | Share | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Proof | 1 per week | Something that happened, with a number. Reel. |
| Teach | 1–2 per week | One thing the viewer can do today. Reel or carousel. |
| Opinion | 1 per week | A position that could lose followers. Reel. |
| Story | 1 per fortnight | A scene with a cost. Reel. |
| Offer | 1 per fortnight | What you sell, said plainly, no apology. Carousel or Stories. |

For each slot give: theme, the specific angle from what actually happened this week, format, and hook formula number. Not a topic — an angle. "AI" is not a plan. "The proposal we lost because the draft had an em dash in it" is a Reel. Mix across the week; never two of the same type back to back.

## When to post
Post when the audience is awake and not at work: early evening local time for most consumer audiences, early morning for a business audience. Anchor to the audience's timezone, not the user's, if those differ. And say this plainly: **the hour matters far less than the first two seconds.** Instagram keeps showing a Reel for days if it performs, and buries a well-timed one that does not. Optimising posting times before the hooks work is polishing the wrong thing.

## The engagement round (not optional)
20 minutes a day, before posting, not after. Build a list of 10:
- **5 reach**: accounts with an audience the user wants, where a good comment gets seen. Comment early, before the thread is 200 deep.
- **3 peers**: same size, same field. The group that reciprocates.
- **2 buyers**: people who could actually buy. Comment for weeks before any DM, and never pitch in a comment.

Hand the list to the engagement workflow.

## Output
A week table (day, time, format, type, hook formula, angle), the Stories note (every day, 3–5 frames), the engagement list, and the line "Say 'write Tuesday' and I will draft it." Write the plan to `~/.claude/instagram/plan.md` so the other workflows can read it. Nothing is scheduled or posted anywhere. This is a plan and the user runs it.
