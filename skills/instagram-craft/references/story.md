# Stories

Stories are not a smaller feed. The feed is how strangers find you. Stories are how the people who already follow you decide whether you are a person worth buying from — and the only surface where a tap turns into a conversation in one move. Nobody outside the followers sees these, so the job is depth, not reach.

## The daily shape
3–7 frames a day. Beyond about seven, the tap-forward rate climbs and the last frames play to nobody — which is exactly where people put the ask.
```
1      THE OPEN    something happening today, with a face or a hand in it.
2–3    THE MIDDLE  the actual content: the process, the result, the mistake.
4      THE ASK     one sticker. Poll, question box, quiz, or link.
5      THE CLOSE   the answer, the outcome, or tomorrow's setup.
```
Put the ask at frame 4, not frame 7. Completion drops with every tap.

## Stickers, and what each is actually for
| Sticker | Job | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Poll | The cheapest tap there is | You want volume of response, not information |
| Question box | Harvesting the exact words people use | You need content ideas or objections, verbatim |
| Quiz | Teaching by letting them be wrong | There is a common misconception in the niche |
| Slider | Vibe check | Nothing important. Fun, low value |
| Link | The only clickable surface besides the bio | There is somewhere real to go |
| Countdown | A deadline people can subscribe to | A launch, a live, a close date |
| Add yours | Reach beyond followers, occasionally | The prompt is one anyone in the niche can answer |

The question box is the most undervalued sticker: every answer is a caption, a Reel hook, or a DM opener written in the audience's own words. Feed them to the Reel workflow as formula #16 (The Verbatim Question).

## Rules
- A face or a hand in the first frame. Text on a coloured background gets tapped through; the first frame decides whether the rest get watched.
- One idea per frame. Reading a paragraph on a Story is not a thing anyone does.
- Keep text inside the middle: on 1080×1920, nothing above y=250 or below y=1600. The profile row sits on top and the reply bar underneath.
- Talk to one person. "You" singular. Stories are the closest thing to a DM that is not one.
- Do not repost your own feed post to Stories with no comment — the single most ignored frame on the platform. If pointing at a post, say what happened in the comments and why it is worth going back for.
- Sell on Stories, not in posts: three frames of context, one frame of offer, one frame of proof. That sequence sells more than a feed post about the same thing and costs no reach.

## The DM funnel, done honestly
A Story that names a problem, a question box or poll that lets people say "that's me", and then a reply to each person who answered. The conversation starts because they spoke first. Automated keyword DM replies are a real, supported feature for professional accounts through Instagram's own tools and approved partners. Bulk DMing people who did not interact is not — it is what gets accounts restricted. The rule is simple: **they act first, then you reply.**

## Output
The frames in order — what is on screen, what is said, which sticker — plus what to do with the answers. Nothing is posted. The user posts it.
