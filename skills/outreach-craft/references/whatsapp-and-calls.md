# WhatsApp and calls — manual text, owner calls, hard caps

## Channel rule (route by what exists)
- **Email where a published email exists.** A published business email is
  the only email route. No published email = no email attempt, no guessed
  address to fill the gap.
- **WhatsApp text where only a phone exists.** This is common in
  local-business outreach: in one recorded day, 0 of 32 leads published a
  usable business email, and on two consecutive days no lead published one
  at all. The published-email gate and the no-website gate are nearly
  mutually exclusive — a lead that needs a website usually has no published
  email either. Plan for the text/call route from the start; do not treat
  "no email found" as a failed day.
- **Call where a verified phone exists and the lead is graded HIGH.**
  Calls are the highest-effort channel; reserve them for the strongest leads.
- **No channel where neither exists.** A lead with no published email and
  no verified phone is uncontactable. Log it as such and move on — do not
  invent a route.

## WhatsApp texts (manual, owner sends)
- **The agent never sends WhatsApp.** Texts are copy-paste drafts for the
  owner to send from his own number. One message per lead, under 500
  characters, verified by count before handing over.
- **Shape:** who you are, one researched observation (same rule as email —
  one true fact), one small ask (a 10-minute call), no pressure. No demo
  link unless the owner has picked one for this batch.
- **One nudge max.** If no reply in 4 days, one polite nudge, then drop.
  No follow-up spam, no second nudge, no re-texting a closed lead.
- **Hours:** never before 9 AM or after 8 PM in the lead's local time.
  Convert the window explicitly (e.g. a US Central 9 AM–8 PM window is
  an evening-to-early-morning window in IST) and state it on the draft.
- **Verification:** the phone number must be verified from a second source
  (listing + another page) before the draft is written. A number from a
  single unverified source is not text-ready.
- **No bulk cold SMS/WhatsApp, ever.** One manual text per lead, from the
  owner's own number, is the whole channel. Bulk blasts are not authorised
  by this skill — the legal risk (US TCPA penalties $500–$1,500 per text)
  is real money per message.

## Call cards (owner calls, agent prepares)
- **One card per lead.** The owner makes the call; the agent prepares
  exactly what he needs to say. No call happens without the owner's
  explicit approval for that call.
- **Card contents:** business name, category, city, verified phone,
  address, one observation (the same researched fact as the text), rating
  / review count if verified, and the one-line opener. Nothing more — a
  card is a prompt, not a script.
- **Call rules:** one call per lead, no retries. Business hours only in
  the lead's local time (e.g. 9 AM–12 PM and 1–5 PM), no weekend calls.
  Disclose AI assistance in the greeting where the call is AI-assisted.
- **After the call, log the outcome the same day** — one of: interested,
  soft-yes, not-interested, gatekeeper, no-answer, wrong-number. A call
  with no logged outcome is an unlogged call; the log is wrong until it
  is fixed.
- **Risk flags on the card, not hidden.** If a recent review alleges
  fraud, food poisoning, or anything that makes the call a reputation
  risk, the card says so and recommends skipping — the owner decides.
  Never soften a risk flag to keep a lead in the queue.

## Grading (honest, per lead)
- Grade HIGH / MEDIUM only on verified evidence: need (no website or
  broken website), recency (dated activity inside the window), phone
  verified, and a named observation. A lead that fails a gate is not
  "MEDIUM to be nice" — it is dropped or watchlisted with the reason
  stated.
- Never fill a quota with weak matches. A short verified list beats a
  long shaky one when every text and call costs reputation.
