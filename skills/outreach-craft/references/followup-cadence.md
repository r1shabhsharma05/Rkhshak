# Follow-up cadence — capped, spaced, and trigger-bound

## The cadence
- **Follow-up 1: day 5 after the first send.** Not day 1, not day 2.
  A reply window of five days is the recorded practice; earlier looks
  desperate, later looks forgotten.
- **Follow-up 2: day 5 after follow-up 1** (day 10 after the first send).
- **Max 2 follow-ups per contact, then close.** No third follow-up, no
  "just bumping this" loop. A closed contact stays closed unless they
  reply — a reply reopens the thread, not the sequence.
- Each follow-up is short — shorter than email one. One sentence of new
  value or one new fact, then the same small ask. Never resend email one
  with "bump" on top. Never add pressure ("third attempt", "closing your
  file").

## Follow-up copy (shape)
- Acknowledge the first mail in one line ("Wrote last week about …").
- Add one thing that was not in email one — a new observation, a shorter
  version of the offer, a different low-friction ask (a yes/no question
  instead of a call).
- Same graceful out. The recipient can decline by ignoring; do not punish
  silence.

## Trigger rules (follow-ups do not reset anything)
- **Follow-ups count toward the send total.** The 30-sends / 0-replies
  trigger counts every send in the stream, follow-ups included. Sending
  follow-ups to a dead template does not buy a new template.
- **If the trigger fires mid-sequence, stop the sequence.** Contacts
  already in a follow-up sequence get no further follow-ups until the
  owner approves the new template. A rewrite proposal replaces the
  sequence, it does not extend it.
- **Bounced contacts get no follow-up.** A hard bounce closes the contact
  for that address immediately (see `deliverability.md`).
- **A reply cancels all pending follow-ups** for that contact at once.
  See `reply-handling.md`.

## Batch discipline
- Follow-ups are due on their day, not in a catch-up blast. If a batch
  of follow-ups was missed, send them on the next send day in the same
  small-batch size — never all at once.
- Log each follow-up with its own date and version, in the same row family
  as the first send. The log must show, per contact: first send date,
  follow-up 1 date, follow-up 2 date, outcome.
- A follow-up sent without a logged first send is a logging error. Fix
  the log before sending more.
