# Local and Maps SEO

## Business type and vertical (detect, never assume)
- **Brick-and-mortar**: customers come to a place. **Service-area business (SAB)**: the business goes to the customer, no public address. **Hybrid**: both.
- Verticals and their signals: restaurant (menu, reservations, cuisine), healthcare (insurance, appointments, credentials), legal (practice areas, bar admission, case results), home services (service area, emergency service, licensed/insured/bonded), real estate (listings, MLS, agent bios), automotive (inventory, VIN, test drives).

## Analysis dimensions (local score 0–100)
1. **GBP signals (~25%)**: Google Business Profile complete — categories, hours, services, photos, attributes, posts, Q&A. An incomplete profile is usually the biggest local fix.
2. **Reviews and reputation (~20%)**: rating, count, velocity (steady beats spiky), response rate and tone. Fake-review signals: bursts of 5-star reviews with no text, identical phrasing, reviewers with one review each.
3. **Local on-page (~20%)**: location pages with genuinely unique content (> 60–70% unique is the working minimum). The swap test: if you can swap the city name and the page still makes sense, it is a doorway page — that pattern has cost sites most of their rankings in core updates.
4. **NAP consistency and citations (~15%)**: name, address, phone identical across the site, schema, GBP, and directories. Check Google, Bing, Apple Maps, and OpenStreetMap — Apple listings are commonly forgotten.
5. **Local schema (~10%)**: correct LocalBusiness subtype for the industry, matching the visible NAP and hours.
6. **Local links and authority (~10%)**: local press, chambers, sponsors, neighbourhood pages — not generic directories.

## AI search impact on local
A growing share of local recommendations now comes through AI answers, not the map pack. The same signals feed both: complete GBP, real reviews, consistent NAP, and a site that states facts an answer engine can cite (address, hours, services, prices). Track AI visibility for local queries as its own KPI, not as a footnote to rankings.

## Maps intelligence
**Geo-grid rank tracking**: scan a keyword from a grid of points around the business (e.g. 7×7) and report share of local voice (SoLV) and average rank. Show the cost estimate and get approval before running a paid grid.
**GBP audit**: field-by-field completeness with industry-specific weights; manual pass when no API tier is available.
**Review intelligence**: cross-platform (Google, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot) — velocity, rating distribution, response rate, and comparison against competitors in the radius.
**Competitor radius mapping**: who is nearby, their rating/review counts, competitive density. Free tier: Overpass API; paid tier: DataForSEO.
**Cross-platform NAP verification**: same business, every platform — report mismatches per field, not a single pass/fail.
**Schema generation**: LocalBusiness JSON-LD built from verified data, never from guessed hours or addresses.

## Output
